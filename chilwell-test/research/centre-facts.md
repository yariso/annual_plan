## Summary

The correct spelling is Chilwell, one "l" in the middle. The user wrote "Chillwell",
which is a common misspelling. Chilwell is a suburban area in the Borough of Broxtowe,
Nottinghamshire, on the west side of Beeston, about 4 miles south west of Nottingham
city centre.

The DVSA calls the centre "Nottingham (Chilwell)", with the town first and the
locality in brackets. That is the string to search for in the GOV.UK booking service.

The centre is NOT on Chetwynd Business Park. Every source found puts it at Unit 24,
Eldon Business Park, Eldon Road, NG9 6DZ. Some listings give the post town as Beeston
and some as Chilwell, both with the same postcode. Chetwynd is a real Chilwell name,
but it belongs to Chetwynd Barracks, a separate Army site about to be redeveloped for
housing, and to Chetwynd Road, where an unrelated MOT garage sits. No source found any
"Chetwynd Business Park" driving test centre.

Eldon Business Park sits just off the A6005 Nottingham Road, adjacent to Chilwell
Retail Park. Immediate neighbours named by instructors are EvoEnergy, Sheetfabs and
NK Motors. The access road off Eldon Road is shared with other businesses.

The car park is small. Instructor accounts describe seven marked bays: five facing the
entrance on the right as you drive in, and two on the left set perpendicular and
bounded by a kerb. The bays are not equal widths, the widest being roughly 10 per cent
wider than the narrowest. Reverse bay parking is normally only set at a test centre car
park, so this is the car park to practise in. Sources conflict on whether on-site
parking is available to candidates; on-street parking on Eldon Road is described as
permitted.

Toilets (male and female, with accessible provision mentioned) and ramp access are
reported. I could not confirm whether there is a staffed waiting room, how many seats
it has, or whether it is open before 8am and at weekends.

The centre opened in 2016 after the Beeston test centre lease ended, with the last
Beeston test on Tuesday 26 July 2016 and a temporary spell running tests from the
Village Hotel in Chilwell from 1 August 2016.

Nottingham's other DVSA car test centre is Nottingham (Colwick), NG4 2JU, near the
city centre. Watnall, NG16 1JF, serves the north west. Beeston and Clifton both closed
in 2016. Derby (Alvaston), DE21 7AY, is about 12 miles away and is sometimes used as
an alternative.

Reported pass rates for Chilwell range from 41.9 per cent to 49.89 per cent depending
on source and year, mostly below the national figure of about 50 per cent. One source
gives a median wait of 7.7 weeks as of August 2026 against a Great Britain median of
11.1 weeks in July 2026.

### Bay parking and the manoeuvre

There are two types of bay parking: forward bay parking, where you drive in and then
reverse out, which can be performed in any car park including the test centre car
park; and reverse bay parking, where you reverse in and then drive out, which
examiners usually only ask for at the test centre car park.
[Exam Routes, Driving Test Centre Car Parks in 2026](https://examroutes.co.uk/27239/driving-test-centre-car-parks-2026/)
(cited by search, page not opened, unofficial)

Many tests end with a manoeuvre in the test centre car park, most commonly a reverse
bay park, sometimes a forward bay park with a reverse out. You are allowed to make
adjustments throughout the manoeuvre, such as reversing and trying again to correct
your position if needed.
[Exam Routes](https://examroutes.co.uk/27239/driving-test-centre-car-parks-2026/)
(cited by search, page not opened, unofficial)

Practical implication for Chilwell specifically: because the two left hand bays are
kerbed and perpendicular to the main five, and because bay widths vary by up to about
10 per cent, a candidate who has only ever practised in a wide supermarket bay may be
caught out here. Practising in this actual car park, outside test hours and without
obstructing DVSA vehicles, is the obvious preparation. (reasoned)
