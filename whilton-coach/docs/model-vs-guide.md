# The model against the guide, corner by corner

Generated by `tools/model_vs_guide.py` on 2026-09-19 from the app's own comparison (`modelAgreement` in src/app.src.html), so the cards and this page say the same thing.

Across every layout, chicane setting and driver level: 48 of 144 corner readings agree on all three counts; the rest differ on at least one, and the table says which.

For each corner: the pedal (flat, lift or brake) the guide gives the driver level (novice, intermediate or advanced) against the pedal the model's hire kart uses at its limit; where the model starts braking against where the guide's painted zone starts; and which side of the track each is on at the approach (where the braking starts, or 20 m before turn-in), the apex and 10 m after the exit. "Agrees" means all three match within the tolerances (pedal class the same, brake point within 10 m, sides the same or within 1.5 m).

The model is a point mass at its grip limit on an estimated hill, calibrated to one measured hire lap; the guides are written for a person. Where they differ, the guide is the day-one plan and the model shows where the time is. A model that brakes later than the guide's zone is expected: the zones carry margin. A model that brakes where the guide says lift or flat says that corner is at the edge for a hire kart at the limit, and a novice should take the guide's word first.
